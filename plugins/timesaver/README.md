MINDY TimeSaver saves you time by saving every time you asked a question, made a decision or commitment and took an action. It's as simple as saving and loading a project in any app so your AI instantly knows what it needs. No more assembling and managing context!

Use /mindyend to save a session, /mindyload to bring earlier work into the conversation, and /mindysearch to find your saved memories. Your saves stay in your own folder on this computer. To set up, type /install-mts with your access code.

Use /mindyteam to send a bug report, give feedback or ask a question.

Use /mindysave to keep the words of a chat.
