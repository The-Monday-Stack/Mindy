MINDY TimeSaver keeps what you asked, decided and did, so you can pick up earlier work with your AI.

Use /mindyend to save a session, /mindyload to bring earlier work into the conversation, and /mindysearch to find your saved memories. Your saves stay in your own folder on this Mac. Setup needs your access code and /install-mts.

Use /mindyteam to send a bug report, give feedback or ask a question.

Use /mindysave to keep the words of a chat.
